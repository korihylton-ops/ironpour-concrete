Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does a driveway pour take?

Most residential driveways are poured and finished within 2 days, with a further 5-7 days curing before vehicle traffic. We'll confirm the exact schedule at quote stage.

Do you handle council permits?

For most residential jobs, no permit is required. For larger or commercial pours we help prepare the documentation your council needs.

What's included in the warranty?

All standard residential work carries a 7-year workmanship warranty covering cracking and surface defects from installation.

Can you match an existing finish?

Yes — bring a photo or a sample to your quote visit and we'll match the finish and colour as closely as possible.

Do you work weekends?

Weekday pours are standard; weekend work can be arranged for commercial jobs with tight deadlines, subject to availability.
