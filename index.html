Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does a driveway pour take?

Most residential driveways are poured and finished within 2 days, with 5-7 days curing before vehicle traffic.

Do you handle council permits?

For most residential jobs, no permit is required. For larger pours we prepare the documentation your council needs.

What's included in the warranty?

All standard residential work carries a 7-year workmanship warranty covering cracking and surface defects.

Can you match an existing finish?

Yes — bring a photo or sample to your quote visit and we'll match the finish and colour.
